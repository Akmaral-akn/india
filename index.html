05 / Table Manners

How Should
You Eat?

01

You may eat with your hands

In some traditional Indian dining settings, eating with the right hand is customary.

02

Use your right hand

The right hand is traditionally used for eating in many Indian dining contexts.

03

Tear the naan

Tear a small piece of naan and use it to pick up food.

04

Take a reasonable amount

You shouldn't take more food than you can comfortably eat.

05

Respect the food

Avoid wasting food prepared especially for guests.

08 / Remember This

The Golden
Rules.

YOU SHOULD

  • ✓ Arrive around the agreed time.
  • ✓ Greet your hosts politely.
  • ✓ Respect household rules.
  • ✓ Try the food with an open mind.
  • ✓ Use your right hand in traditional settings.
  • ✓ Compliment and thank your host.

YOU SHOULDN'T

  • × Arrive extremely early.
  • × Waste food.
  • × Criticize unfamiliar food.
  • × Take more food than you can eat.
  • × Touch religious objects without permission.
  • × Make disrespectful jokes about traditions.
YOU MUST: respect the host, their home and any specific cultural or religious instructions.
